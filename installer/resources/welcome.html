Welcome to TouchGuard Menu Bar

TouchGuard Menu Bar stops accidental trackpad taps while you type. After each key press it briefly ignores clicks, so a palm brushing the trackpad can't move your text cursor.

After installing:

  1. TouchGuard Menu Bar starts automatically and appears as a hand icon in the menu bar.
  2. macOS will ask you to allow Accessibility access. Turn on TouchGuard Menu Bar in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  3. Optional: choose Launch at Login from the menu bar icon to start it automatically.

To uninstall, choose Quit TouchGuard Menu Bar from the menu, then move TouchGuard Menu Bar from Applications to the Trash.
